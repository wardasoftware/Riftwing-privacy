Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Welcome to RiftWing, a mobile arcade game published by WardaSoft ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use RiftWing.

1. Information and Game Data

RiftWing may process information in the following ways, depending on how you use the game and the services enabled in the app.

Information such as game progress, scores, and game settings may be stored on your device to support gameplay and remember your preferences. This information may be lost if you clear the app's data or uninstall the game, unless a supported backup or synchronization feature is available and enabled.

We do not operate our own game account system or collect personal information through a registration form unless such a feature is introduced in a future version.

Advertising information

RiftWing uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on your device settings, location, applicable consent requirements, and the advertising configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, app activity, diagnostics, and related technical information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, prevent fraud, and meet legal requirements.

The information processed by advertising services is subject to the applicable Google policies and settings. We do not control all information collected directly by Google or its advertising partners.

RiftWing uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. These services may process information on their own systems in accordance with their applicable policies.

Information processed by RiftWing and its enabled third-party services may be used to:

  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the game.

We do not sell personal information collected directly by us in exchange for money. Information may be processed or shared by third-party service providers, including Google AdMob, as necessary to provide advertising and related services. RiftWing is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account-registration system.

The game uses third-party advertising services, which may process device or usage information. Advertising settings, SDK configuration, and any age-related requirements must be configured appropriately for the intended audience.

If you believe that information has been collected inappropriately in connection with RiftWing, please contact us using the details below.
